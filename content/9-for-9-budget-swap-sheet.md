## The same 9 items, a cleaner cart

Most of us buy the same handful of things every week, so those few items matter more than anything else in the cart. Change them once and every shop after that gets cleaner by itself.

Here is the trouble. The best-known brands often carry the longest labels: corn syrup in the ketchup, two artificial sweeteners in the chocolate milk, three petroleum-based dyes in a breakfast pastry.

None of that is on the front of the box. The front is for selling. The back is where the truth lives, in small print, written for nobody to read.

You do not need a bigger grocery bill to fix it. For each of these 9 brands, there is a cleaner pick a few feet away, often in the same aisle. This sheet names it, tells you about what it costs, and where to find it.

:::warn Three lines to find on the back of any pack
Look for **high fructose corn syrup**, any **number dye** such as Red 40, Yellow 6 or Blue 1, and **sucralose** or **acesulfame potassium**, two zero-calorie sweeteners. If a label holds one of them, it is worth a swap. Each item below names the cleaner pick and where to buy it.
:::

## Swap 1: Kirkland Signature rotisserie chicken

Rotisserie chicken feels like the healthy shortcut, which is why this swap matters. The Costco bird costs [$4.99](https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/33047-lawsuit-challenges-costcos-no-preservatives-labeling-on-rotisserie-chicken), a price that has not moved since 2009.

**The usual pick:** the seasoning on the Kirkland bird adds [salt, sodium phosphate, modified food starch, carrageenan, sugar and dextrose](https://www.tastingtable.com/2116507/costco-rotisserie-chicken-preservatives-list). Sodium phosphate is a salt that helps meat hold water. Carrageenan is a thickener made from seaweed. A 2026 lawsuit also [challenges](https://www.meatpoultry.com/articles/33047-lawsuit-challenges-costcos-no-preservatives-labeling-on-rotisserie-chicken) its "no preservatives" claim.

**The cleaner swap:** roast your own. Kirkland Signature Organic Whole Chickens come two to a pack at Costco, at about $2.49 to $2.99 a pound, with no added salt. At Aldi, the [Never Any! whole chicken](https://www.aldi.us/products/fresh-meat-seafood/fresh-poultry/detail/ps/p/never-any-fresh-whole-chicken) is raised with no antibiotics and no added hormones.

![Usual pick: a glossy rotisserie chicken in a plastic dome. Cleaner swap: a golden home-roasted chicken in a cast iron skillet with lemon, garlic and thyme.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-chicken-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Costco (Kirkland Signature Organic), Aldi (Never Any!).

**The budget truth:** one organic bird costs more than the $4.99 rotisserie, but it feeds you twice. Roast it Sunday, eat it Monday, and boil the bones for broth.

## Swap 2: Heinz Tomato Ketchup

Ketchup goes on almost everything kids eat, so a small change here adds up fast. Heinz lists [high fructose corn syrup and corn syrup](https://www.heinz.com/products/00013000006408) as its third and fourth ingredients, with 4 grams of added sugar in every tablespoon.

**The cleaner swap:** [Primal Kitchen Organic Unsweetened Ketchup](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/B07DL78BF5). It is made from tomato concentrate, balsamic vinegar, salt and spices, with no added sugar and 105 mg of sodium per tablespoon. It costs about $5.50 for an 11.3-ounce bottle at Walmart, and Target carries the squeeze bottle.

**On a tighter budget:** the store-brand organic ketchups, Good & Gather Organic at Target and Simply Nature Organic at Aldi, swap the corn syrup for organic sugar. That removes the corn syrup but not the sugar, so treat them as a step up, not the finish line.

![Usual pick: Heinz Tomato Ketchup. Cleaner swap: Primal Kitchen Organic Unsweetened Ketchup.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-ketchup-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart and Target (Primal Kitchen), Target (Good & Gather Organic), Aldi (Simply Nature Organic).

## Swap 3: fairlife chocolate milk

fairlife sells itself on protein, and it does deliver 13 grams a cup. The sweetness is the catch. Its label lists [sucralose, acesulfame potassium and carrageenan](https://www.fairlife.com/products/chocolate-milk/) next to the sugar.

**The cleaner swap:** [Horizon Organic Reduced Fat Chocolate Milk](https://milams.instacart.com/store/milams/products/28368584-horizon-organic-2-chocolate-milk-59-fl-oz). It is organic milk, cane sugar and cocoa, with no artificial sweeteners and no carrageenan. Here is the honest part: it has 23 grams of sugar a cup, against fairlife's 12. Pour a small glass, and keep it a treat.

**The cheapest swap of all:** stir a spoon of plain cocoa and a little maple syrup into the milk you already buy. You choose the sugar, and you pay nothing extra.

![Usual pick: fairlife Chocolate 2% milk. Cleaner swap: Horizon Organic Chocolate 2% milk.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-chocolate-milk-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart and Target (Horizon Organic).

## Swap 4: Jif Creamy Peanut Butter

Peanut butter needs one ingredient, so every extra is a choice the company made. [Jif Creamy](https://www.jif.com/products/creamy/creamy-peanut-butter) adds sugar, molasses, salt and fully hydrogenated vegetable oils, which are oils made solid so the jar never separates.

**The cleaner swap:** [Smucker's Natural Creamy](https://www.smuckers.com/peanut-butter/natural/natural-creamy-peanut-butter), made from peanuts and a pinch of salt. It costs about $4 a jar at Walmart and is stocked at Target. [Crazy Richard's](https://www.walmart.com/ip/20683208) is peanuts and nothing else, at about $3.50 a jar.

**At Costco and Aldi:** Kirkland Signature Organic Creamy is organic peanuts and sea salt. Aldi's Simply Nature Organic Creamy is the same two ingredients, at about $4.50 a jar.

**Tip:** natural peanut butter separates. Stir it once, then keep it in the fridge and it stays mixed.

![Usual pick: Jif Creamy Peanut Butter. Cleaner swap: Smucker's Natural Creamy Peanut Butter.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-peanut-butter-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart and Target (Smucker's Natural, Crazy Richard's), Costco (Kirkland Signature Organic), Aldi (Simply Nature Organic).

## Swap 5: Kraft Singles

Look closely at the wrapper and you will see the words "pasteurized prepared cheese product," not cheese. That name has [no federal standard](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-133/subpart-B) at all. Even "process cheese food" has to be at least [51 percent cheese](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-133/subpart-B/section-133.173).

**The usual pick:** [Kraft Singles](https://www.kraftheinz.com/kraft-singles/products) list cheddar, then skim milk, whey, milkfat, milk protein concentrate, calcium phosphate, sodium phosphate, modified food starch and a preservative. That is about 15 ingredients for one slice.

**The cleaner swap:** [Tillamook Medium Cheddar slices](https://bashas.instacart.com/store/bashas/products/83266-tillamook-farmstyle-thick-cut-medium-cheddar-cheese-slices-8-oz). The label reads cultured milk, salt, enzymes and annatto, a color from a seed. Expect about $6 for an 8-ounce pack. Cabot and Organic Valley slices are just as simple.

![Usual pick: Kraft Singles American. Cleaner swap: Tillamook Medium Cheddar slices.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-cheese-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart, Target and Costco carry Tillamook. For the lowest price, buy a block and slice it yourself.

## Swap 6: Coca-Cola

Soda is the single biggest source of sugar for many families, so this swap moves the needle most. One 12-ounce can of Coke holds [39 grams of sugar](https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/about-us/faq/how-much-sugar-is-in-coca-cola). The new [federal dietary guidelines](https://www.dietaryguidelines.gov/) say no amount of added sugar is recommended, and no meal should go over 10 grams.

**The cleaner swap:** [Olipop Vintage Cola](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/B07QF9H7K2), sweetened with cassava root syrup, fruit juice and stevia, with 2 to 3 grams of sugar and 6 to 9 grams of fiber a can. A 12-pack runs about $26 to $29 at Walmart, and Target and Costco carry it too.

**For zero sugar:** [Zevia Cola](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/B09XTH44JJ) is sweetened with stevia, a sweetener from a plant leaf, and skips the caramel color. A 24-pack is about $36 at Walmart.

![Usual pick: Coca-Cola Original. Cleaner swaps: Olipop Vintage Cola and Zevia Cola.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-cola-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart, Target and Costco (Olipop), Walmart and Target (Zevia).

## Swap 7: Pop-Tarts Frosted Strawberry

This is the one with the dyes, and it is often a child's breakfast. [Pop-Tarts Frosted Strawberry](https://henhouse.instacart.com/store/hen-house/products/20391806) list corn syrup, high fructose corn syrup, TBHQ, a preservative, and Red 40, Yellow 6 and Blue 1.

The FDA asked food makers in April 2025 to [remove those exact dyes](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/hhs-fda-phase-out-petroleum-based-synthetic-dyes-nations-food-supply) by the end of 2026. Until the box changes, you can change the box.

**The cleaner swap:** [Nature's Path Organic Frosted Berry Strawberry Toaster Pastries](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/B000VK66G6). The pink comes from red beet powder, with no synthetic dyes, no TBHQ and no corn syrup. A box of 6 is about $5.39.

**The honest part:** these pastries carry 38 grams of sugar for two, more than the original. They are a cleaner treat, not a health food. Serve one, not two, and keep them for weekends.

![Usual pick: Pop-Tarts Frosted Strawberry. Cleaner swap: Nature's Path Organic Frosted Berry Strawberry Toaster Pastries.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-toaster-pastries-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart and Target (Nature's Path).

## Swap 8: Nature's Own Honey Wheat

"Honey wheat" sounds like whole grain, so it is worth checking what comes first. [Nature's Own Honey Wheat](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/B000LR4XJ6) lists wheat flour, which is refined white flour, ahead of whole wheat. Each slice has just 1 gram of fiber.

**The cleaner swap:** [Food for Life Ezekiel 4:9](https://www.foodforlife.com/product/breads/ezekiel-49-sprouted-whole-grain-bread), made from sprouted grains and lentils, with no added sugar and 3 grams of fiber a slice. Sprouted means the grain started to grow before baking. Find it in the freezer aisle at Target and Walmart.

**The softer swap:** [Dave's Killer Bread 21 Whole Grains and Seeds](https://www.daveskillerbread.com/21-whole-grains-and-seeds) is organic whole wheat first, with 5 grams of fiber a slice. It also has 5 grams of sugar, so it is better for fiber than for sugar. It costs about $6.50 a loaf at Walmart.

![Usual pick: Nature's Own Honey Wheat. Cleaner swaps: Food for Life Ezekiel 4:9 and Dave's Killer Bread 21 Whole Grains and Seeds.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-bread-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart and Target (Ezekiel in the freezer, Dave's Killer Bread on the shelf), Costco (Dave's Killer Bread twin packs in many warehouses).

## Swap 9: Goldfish Cheddar Crackers

Goldfish get the color right: the orange comes from annatto, not a dye. The swap is about the base. [Goldfish Cheddar](https://henhouse.instacart.com/store/hen-house/products/16409459-pepperidge-farm-goldfish-cheddar-crackers-6-6-oz) are refined enriched flour and a blend of canola, soybean or sunflower oil, plus autolyzed yeast extract, a flavor booster.

**The cleaner swap:** [Simple Mills Farmhouse Cheddar](https://www.simplemills.com/products/farmhouse-cheddar-almond-flour-crackers) crackers, made from almonds, sunflower seeds and flax, with organic cheddar and no refined flour. A box is about $5 to $6.

**The kid-friendly swap:** [Annie's Organic Cheddar Bunnies](https://www.wholefoodsmarket.com/grocery/product/B076HRWTK2) use organic flour and organic cheddar. The sodium is about the same as Goldfish, so this is a cleaner-ingredients swap, not a lower-salt one. A bag is about $5 to $7 at Walmart.

![Usual pick: Goldfish Cheddar. Cleaner swaps: Simple Mills Farmhouse Cheddar crackers and Annie's Organic Cheddar Bunnies.](assets/guides/9-for-9-budget-swap-sheet-crackers-1.jpg)

**Where to buy:** Walmart, Target and Costco (Annie's, Simple Mills).

## Your next shop, 9 swaps at a time

:::protocol
You do not have to switch all 9 this week. Start with what you buy most often, and let the rest follow.

1. **Pick your 3.** Circle the three items from this list that land in your cart every week.
2. **Start with the drink.** If soda or chocolate milk is one of them, swap it first. It moves the most sugar.
3. **Check one back label per aisle.** Look for corn syrup, a number dye or sucralose. If you see one, use the swap from this sheet.
4. **Buy the store brand where it passes.** Kirkland and Simply Nature organic peanut butter are the same two ingredients as the name brands, for less.
5. **Cook the chicken once.** Roast one whole bird this weekend and plan two meals from it.
:::

Every swap you make is one less label to second-guess next week. Start with the three items you buy most, and let the rest of the cart follow.
