01 — The Mission

biology you
can read.

Lifeuntox Labs exists to translate the science of biological aging and toxic exposure into something an ordinary person can hold, read, and act on.

For the last decade, the most powerful diagnostic tools in modern medicine have lived inside research labs and elite longevity clinics. Lifeuntox Labs takes those tools out of the institution and puts them into a guided programme designed for the people who have spent years cleaning up their lives without ever being given a way to see whether it worked.

We are not a longevity brand. We are a measurement brand for people who already understand that the modern environment is aging them. We turn invisible biological processes into a number, a score, and a plan.

Wherever you are in this journey, your body has been keeping score. It is time to see the scoreboard. The Lifeuntox Labs Promise

Our Four Pillars

01 / Measurement
measurable, not anecdotal.

Every claim, every protocol, every result is anchored to a number you can read on a report. We do not sell feelings. We sell data your biology has been keeping the entire time.

02 / Translation
science you can hold.

The research we use was built for academic journals. Our job is to translate it into a programme you can complete in 90 days, in your own home, without a medical degree.

03 / Cohorts
together, in waves.

We do not sell rolling enrolment. We open cohorts. People move through the programme alongside others, with the same questions, the same reveal moments, and the same support.

04 / Sovereignty
your data is yours.

Every score, every report, every protocol belongs to the person it was built for. We never sell, share, or aggregate data without permission. Trust is the only product we cannot afford to lose.

02 — Brand Relationship

part of the family.
distinct in the room.

03 — The Logomark

the leaf, alive.

A pair of green leaves emerging from a black field, beside the wordmark. The leaf is borrowed from the parent Lifeuntox identity but rendered in living, dimensional colour rather than flat illustration. The black field signals the laboratory. The wordmark is set in League Spartan Bold lowercase, with "labs" anchoring it as a sub-brand of the Lifeuntox parent.

Always

Use on dark backgrounds first. The leaf was designed to feel alive against laboratory black. The dimensionality of the gradient depends on the depth of the field behind it.

Permitted

Inverse use on cream or warm white is allowed for editorial moments where the parent brand context dominates. Use the inverted file, not a recoloured one.

Never

Do not flatten the leaf into a single colour. Do not stretch, rotate, or recolour the wordmark. Do not pair the logomark with any background lighter than #faf8f3 or busier than 5% noise.

The 60·30·10 Rule

Lab Black dominates. Forest carries structure. Leaf appears only at moments of life, growth, or revealed data. Signal Green appears once per page, never twice.

Signal Green = Data

Signal Green is reserved exclusively for the moment a number appears. A biological age. A score. A reveal. Never used decoratively. Always carries meaning.

Gold = Premium · Red = Warning

Gold appears only on lifetime Premium membership cues. Red appears only on toxin warnings, recall alerts, and Blacklist entries. Functional only. Never decorative.

06 — Voice & Tone

clinical, but human.

Where Lifeuntox the parent brand is warm, activist, and editorial, Lifeuntox Labs is precise, considered, and clinical. The fire from the parent brand is still here. It just speaks more quietly, with the authority of someone who has measured what they are talking about. Read the lab notes. Then react.

Do · The Labs Voice

"In the 2024 NHANES cohort, cadmium exposure alone accounted for 43 to 46 percent of the biological aging gap between adults living otherwise identical lifestyles."

Specific. Sourced. Restrained. The reader does the emotional work because the data is doing the heavy lifting. We do not need to underline the importance.

Don't · The Wellness Voice

"Heavy metals are SHOCKING your body and SECRETLY aging you faster than you ever imagined! The toxin truth they don't want you to know!"

Hyperbolic. Unsourced. Loud. This is the voice of every wellness Instagram account that gets ignored after the third reel. Lifeuntox Labs sounds nothing like this.

Do · The Labs Voice

"Your biological age came back at 38.4. Your chronological age is 43. The gap is 4.6 years younger than the calendar. Here is what to do next."

Number. Context. Direction. Three sentences. The reader knows what was measured, what it means, and what comes next.

Don't · The Coach Voice

"Wow, your biological age is amazing! You're crushing it! Keep going on this incredible journey of self-optimisation, queen!"

Performative. Validating. Empty. Lifeuntox Labs never confuses cheerleading with empowerment. We respect the reader enough to let the data speak.

07 — Imagery System

three visual registers.

Lifeuntox Labs operates across three visual registers, each doing a specific job. The laboratory register signals measurement and precision. The measurement register translates data into something the reader can hold. The molecular register communicates what is happening at a level the eye cannot see.

REGISTER 01
laboratory.
Black field with subtle grid. Single point of light. The instrument view.
REGISTER 02
measurement.
Cream paper. Lined ground. Data presented as legible artifact.
REGISTER 03
molecular.
Forest field. Green points of light. The biology view.

Imagery Principles

01

Show the instrument, not the influencer.

We do not feature wellness personalities, before-and-after bodies, or stylised lifestyle imagery. We show the report, the lab, the molecule, the data. The reader is the protagonist, never the model.

02

Black is the canvas. Green is the life.

Imagery defaults to dark backgrounds. Green lives inside that darkness as the colour of the leaf, the data point, the signal. Reverse this only when the parent Lifeuntox tone needs to be invoked.

03

Data is design.

A number set in JetBrains Mono on a black field is the most powerful image we own. Numbers do not need to be illustrated. They need to be staged.

04

If we cannot source it, we do not show it.

Stock imagery is permitted only when no editorial alternative exists. No fake test results. No mocked-up labs. No invented researchers. Authenticity is the entire product.

08 — Data System

numbers, staged.

Every Lifeuntox Labs programme produces numbers. The visual treatment of those numbers is one of the most important aspects of the brand. A score is not a graphic. It is the product. The way a score is rendered changes whether the reader believes it.
